Callie on your phone

Callie runs in your phone's browser, so there is no app store and nothing to download. It needs a Callie link that starts with https://. Whoever runs Callie gives you that link or a QR code.

1. Open the link

Scan the QR code, or tap the link you were sent. Allow the microphone when your phone asks.

2. Put it on your home screen

PhoneDo this
Android (Chrome)Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
iPhone (Safari)Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

3. Make a call

Tap a contact and talk. Headphones give the best result. If Callie says it cannot hear you, open Settings > Microphone and press Allow microphone, then Test.

To keep the free service available for everyone, each phone can start 5 calls a day. A call that ends within a few seconds does not count.

Running Callie yourself?

To get a link for your own phone from your computer, follow Use it on your phone in the guide. To keep Callie online all the time for other people, see Hosting Callie.
