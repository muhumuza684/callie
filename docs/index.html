Call an AI by voice, like phoning a friend.

Pick a contact, hear it ring, and just talk. Each contact has its own name, voice and language, and every call is clear that you are talking to an AI.

Download CallieRead the guide

As simple as a phone call

No typing, no menus once you are connected.

1

Pick someone

Callie, or a contact you create with a name, a voice and a language.

2

Tap to call

It rings, they answer. Interrupt them any time, like a real conversation.

3

Just talk

Practise a language, think out loud, or enjoy some company.

Made for real conversation

The details that make it feel natural.

8 languages

English, Kiswahili, Francais, Espanol, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi and Portuguese.

9 voice types

Female, male, girl, boy, baby, adult, old lady, old man and stranger.

Calls that call you

Schedule a time and Callie rings you like an incoming call.

Remembers, if you want

Switch memory on to continue where you left off. Off by default.

Looks the way you like

Six themes and five ringtones, or your phone's own sound.

Private by design

No recordings are kept, and the AI always says it is an AI.
